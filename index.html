About

Owner-operated since 2012

Urban Canopy Tree Services has provided safe tree removal and limbing to homeowners and businesses in Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Stoney Creek, Dundas and beyond since 2012.

Urban Canopy is owned by Gavin Sadler and operated with a small group of employees. As a local, independent business, we care that every customer is happy with the result.

  • Insured through private insurance and the Workplace Safety and Insurance Board
  • ISA member of the International Society of Arboriculture
  • Dependable: we respect your time and property
  • Professional: courteous, with honest advice when quoting and working

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are you insured?

Yes. The owner, Gavin Sadler, is insured through private insurance, and crew employees are covered by the Workplace Safety and Insurance Board.

How much will tree removal cost?

The cost depends on the size of the tree, the number of trees and how difficult the access is. Tree removal starts at $200 per tree. Contact us for a written quote.

Can you provide references?

Yes, we would be happy to provide references. Call 905-912-8876, or read our customers' reviews on YellowPages.ca.

Can I get a written quote?

Yes. Get in touch to book a time for us to come to the property and provide one.
